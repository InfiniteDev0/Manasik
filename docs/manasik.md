One product suggestion

Instead of exposing every module immediately, build role-based workspaces.

For example:

An Operations Manager logs in and sees bookings, groups, hotels, transport, and tasks.
A Finance user sees payments, invoices, refunds, and reports.
A Guide only sees their assigned groups, attendance, announcements, and schedules.
The Owner gets the full business dashboard.

That keeps the interface clean and makes the platform feel tailored to each user's job instead of overwhelming everyone with every feature.
